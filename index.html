Blank detects it from the data.
Balance before the first row in your data; deposits inside the data are added on top. Turns on % return, drawdown as % of balance and risk of ruin.
When you adjust a stop after entry, the last one keeping at least this much of the original risk is treated as your real risk. Tighter moves are read as banking profit.
Rescales with every deposit instead of being a fixed amount. At 50%, two full-size losses ends a session. Blank turns the check off.
For the rolling win rate chart. Blank picks one from your sample size.
Applies everywhere: charts, calendar shading, badges and exported images. Red and green are the hardest pair to tell apart for the most common form of colour blindness, so blue/orange is offered as an alternative.
Comma-separated. Assign them from the Setup column in the trade table; the Performance section then breaks P&L down by setup.
Anything that answers with Liquid's own shapes — positions, rows, or both. Polled while the Liquid book is open. Leave it blank and you paste instead.
Sent as a bearer token, if your endpoint wants one.
Positions move with the price; realised history barely changes. 15s is plenty.
Reads history only. Not your IG password, which stays at Cloudflare.
Auto-refresh open positionsPolls only the positions endpoint. A full history sync runs when a position closes, when you return to the tab, and every five minutes.
Used only for positions priced in another currency. Nothing is converted unless you supply the rate, so the total is never based on a number this app guessed.
Enables the Close button on open positions. Must match CLOSE_TOKEN on your worker, and must differ from the sync token. Leave blank and you will be prompted per close instead of storing it.
Enables New order, which opens positions. Deliberately a different secret from the close token: a leaked close token can only shut positions, one that could also open them is a far bigger problem. Must match ORDER_TOKEN on your worker.
Minimum 2s. IG allows roughly 40 requests a minute; 2s uses 30 and leaves headroom.
Files accumulate rather than replace, so several exports can be layered to cover a longer history.
Hide weekends on the calendarSaturday and Sunday columns are dropped. If a weekend ever has activity, it is shown anyway so nothing disappears.
A new deposit starts a new sessionOn: topping up mid-session closes the old one and carries the remaining balance into a new session. Off: deposits accumulate and only a withdrawal ends a session.
Include fees & funding in net P&LOvernight funding, dividends and stop premiums. They never count toward win rate.
